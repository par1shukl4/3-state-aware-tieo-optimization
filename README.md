# Three-State-Aware TIEO Optimization

**Developed and Modelled by Pari Shukla and Nitya Nand**

### Quantile-Based Q-Learning with Powell Local Refinement

An extension of the original TIEO optimization framework, introducing a three-state representation for Q-learning, quantile-based learning, and Powell optimization for local refinement.

## Overview

This project explores an extension of the original TIEO optimization framework by modifying its Q-learning component and incorporating an additional local optimization stage.

We initially studied and understood the original TIEO methodology, which employed a two-state representation for its Q-learning component. We subsequently extended this representation to three states and incorporated a quantile-based approach into the learning process. Finally, Powell optimization was employed for local refinement of the obtained solution.

The objective of this work is to investigate the impact of an extended state representation, quantile-based learning, and local optimization on the performance of the original TIEO framework.

## Objectives

- Study and understand the original TIEO optimization framework.
- Extend the Q-learning state representation from two states to three states.
- Incorporate quantile-based learning into the modified Q-learning formulation.
- Apply Powell optimization for local refinement of candidate solutions.
- Evaluate the modified framework against the original baseline.

## Methodology

### 1. Original TIEO Framework

The original TIEO framework serves as the baseline for this work. Its optimization methodology and Q-learning formulation were studied to understand the underlying decision-making process and optimization mechanism.

The baseline framework employs a two-state representation in its Q-learning component.

### 2. Three-State Extension

The original two-state representation was extended to a three-state representation.

This modification introduces an additional state into the learning framework, providing a richer representation of the system and enabling the agent to distinguish between additional conditions during the optimization process.

### 3. Quantile-Based Q-Learning

Quantile-based learning was incorporated into the modified three-state Q-learning framework.

This approach explores distribution-aware learning by considering quantile information during the learning and decision-making process, rather than relying exclusively on conventional value estimates.

The quantile-based formulation is integrated into the extended state representation to investigate its influence on optimization performance.

### 4. Powell Optimization for Local Refinement

Following the Q-learning-based optimization stage, Powell optimization is employed for local refinement.

Powell's method is a derivative-free numerical optimization technique that iteratively searches along selected directions to improve a candidate solution. It is particularly useful when gradient information is unavailable or difficult to obtain.

In this work, Powell optimization is used as an additional refinement stage to improve the candidate solution generated by the learning-based optimization process.
